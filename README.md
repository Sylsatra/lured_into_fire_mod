## They See The Light…

Ever felt your brightly lit base was a little _too_ safe? **Item & Block Attraction** is a simple yet transformative mod that overhauls hostile mob AI, making them irresistibly drawn to light sources. That flickering torch is no longer just a tool for vision; it's a beacon for every monster lurking in the dark on your **Forge 1.20.1** server or single-player world.

This mod fundamentally changes how you approach lighting, defense, and even farming. Use this new behavior to your advantage to create hyper-efficient mob traps, or fight for your life as your once-safe base becomes a magnet for danger.

***

## Key Features

*   🔦 **Intelligent Mob Attraction:** Hostile mobs will now actively detect and pathfind towards any block or item that emits light. They aren't just wandering anymore—they're hunting the light!
*   🧠 **Smarter Mob AI for 1.20.1:** Creates more dynamic and challenging encounters. Mobs will swarm your illuminated defenses, forcing you to rethink your base-building strategies in the latest version of Minecraft.
*   🚜 **Ultimate Mob Farming:** Revolutionize your mob farms! Use a single torch or lantern as bait to lure mobs into a central grinder. Create the most efficient mob trap with minimal effort.
*   🧟 **Fungal Infection: Spore Integration:** The terrifying infected creatures from the **Fungal Infection: Spore** mod are also drawn to the light, seamlessly integrating this mod into your horror-survival playthroughs.

### FAQ

**Q: Is this for Fabric or other versions like 1.19?** A: No. As stated at the top, this mod is **exclusively for Forge 1.20.1**. There are no plans for other versions or loaders at this time.

**Q: Does this work with mobs from other mods?** A: Yes! You can add any mob to the attraction list using the `light_attracted_mobs` data pack tag.

**Q: Is it safe to add to an existing world?** A: Yes, it is perfectly safe to add or remove from an existing Forge 1.20.1 world.

**Q: Will this cause lag?** A: The mod is optimized to have a minimal performance impact. The AI change is lightweight and shouldn't cause server lag unless you have thousands of mobs pathfinding at once.
